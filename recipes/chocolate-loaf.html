Chocolate Swirl Loaf

A soft cocoa dough rolled around silky chocolate ganache, cut and twisted so the layers show on every side.

Adapted from 蔚然🥨’s chocolate loaf on Xiaohongshu, then tested and adjusted in Bunny’s kitchen.

  • 2 loavesabout 500 g each
  • ≈ 4½ hrplus ganache made ahead
  • 325°Fbake about 30 min

Ingredients

2 loaves

Ganache filling

Dough

Optional extras

Chocolate chips or crushed OreosFor sprinkling inside and on top, to tastea handful

Each loaf is made from two pieces of dough baked side by side in one pan.

Ingredient guide → which milk, eggs, butter, flour, and yeast to use.

Make the ganache ahead

  1. Make the ganache

    Combine the cream and chocolate chips and heat in the microwave until the chocolate melts, then stir until smooth and thick. Chill it in the fridge so it firms up a little.

    Make it at least 3 hours ahead. Chilled ganache spreads much more easily than warm, runny ganache.
    At least 3 hr before shaping

Make the dough

  1. Make the cocoa paste

    Stir the cocoa powder with the hot water into a very, very thick paste.

    Why a paste? Mixing the cocoa with hot water first keeps it from weakening the gluten, especially with Dutch-process (alkalized) cocoa. If you’re using natural, non-alkalized cocoa, you can skip this step and add the cocoa and water straight into the dough.

  2. Mix and rest

    Combine everything for the dough except the butter and salt: flour, cocoa paste, sugar, egg, milk, and yeast. Mix roughly until no dry flour remains, then cover and let it rest so the flour can hydrate.

    Rest about 30 min

  3. Knead in butter and salt

    Knead until the dough forms a rough film when stretched. Add the butter and salt and keep kneading until it reaches about 90% of a full windowpane: very thin and stretchy, close to see-through.

  4. Divide and first proof

    Divide the dough into 4 equal pieces, two per loaf. Round each into a ball, cover, and let them rise somewhere warm.

    About 40 min

Fill and shape

Shape one piece at a time, keeping the rest covered.

  1. Roll out

    Roll one piece of dough into a square about 8 × 8 inches.

  2. Spread the ganache

    Stir the chilled ganache until silky, then spread it over the dough. Sprinkle on some chocolate chips or crushed Oreos if you like.

  3. Roll into a log

    Roll the dough up into a log, pinch the seam closed, and press it down a little to flatten slightly.

  4. Cut into three strands

    Leaving the top end of the log uncut, cut the rest lengthwise into 3 strands. Turn each strand so the cut side faces down.

  5. Roll up and pan

    Starting from the uncut end, roll the dough up toward the strands. Place two rolls side by side in each loaf pan.

Bake

  1. Final proof

    Cover and let the loaves rise in a warm spot until well risen in the pans. Preheat the oven to 325°F toward the end.

    About 1½–2 hr

  2. Top and bake

    Sprinkle the tops with chocolate chips or crushed Oreos, if you like. Bake on the lower-middle rack at 325°F.

    About 30 min at 325°F

  3. Unmold and cool

    Turn the loaves out of the pans and let them cool on a rack.
